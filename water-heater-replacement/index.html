Water Heater Replacement in Thornhill

Old tank leaking or out of hot water? Here's how replacement works.

Water heater replacement swaps your old tank for a new one and reconnects the gas, water, venting and safety valve. In Thornhill it's the usual answer when a tank leaks, since a leaking tank body can't be repaired.

This page covers signs it's time, sizing, venting, rented tanks, tank versus tankless and how the day goes.

How do I know it's time?

Most tanks last roughly 8 to 15 years. The date is on the label.

  • Water pooling at the base of the tank

  • Rusty hot water

  • Rumbling or banging that flushing doesn't fix

  • Corrosion on the tank or fittings

  • Repeated repairs, or never enough hot water

How is the new one sized?

By how much hot water your household uses, not just by the size of the old tank. Too small and you run out. Too big wastes gas.

Fill out the form, or call and we'll book it with you.

What does the install need?

A gas water heater needs proper venting to the outside, and in a finished space a drain pan to catch leaks. We'll tell you which permits apply.

Is my tank rented?

Many Ontario homes rent their water heater. If yours is rented, the rental company usually handles repairs and the replacement at end of life, so read the contract before you buy. Rental fees can add up to more than a tank is worth over the years, so compare the numbers.

Tank or tankless?

A tank is simpler and costs less to install. A tankless unit gives endless hot water and takes less space, but needs the right gas line and venting. See tankless installation.

How does a replacement go?

  1. Shut off and drain the old tank.
  2. Install the new tank with a new relief valve and fittings.
  3. Reconnect gas and venting and test the gas connection.
  4. Fill, light and test the heater.
