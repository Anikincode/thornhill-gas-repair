Heat Pump Maintenance in Thornhill

A heat pump works in both seasons, so it needs a check twice a year.

Heat pump maintenance is a regular inspection and clean of the indoor and outdoor units, the coils, the drain, the electrical parts and the refrigerant. In Thornhill we suggest checking it before each heating and cooling season.

This page covers what's checked, why a heat pump gets two visits a year, the signs it's overdue and what you can do between visits.

What do you check?

  • Outdoor coil and fan

  • Indoor coil, filter and drain

  • Electrical connections and the defrost control

  • Refrigerant and temperature split

Why twice a year?

A heat pump works in both seasons, so it's worth a check before each one: spring for cooling, fall for heating. The defrost control matters most before winter.

What are the signs it's overdue?

For ice that won't clear, see heat pump repair.

  • Ice on the outdoor unit that doesn't clear

  • Weak heating or cooling

  • Water or ice around the indoor unit

What can I do myself?

Keep snow, leaves and debris off the outdoor unit and change the filter on schedule. Don't chip ice off the coil.
