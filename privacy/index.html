Privacy

What this site collects, why, and who sees it, in plain words. Updated November 2026.

What do we collect?

  • What you send us. Your name, phone number, address and message, if you fill in the form. When you call, we take the details we need to book the job.
  • What your browser shares. Like most websites, our host may log technical details such as your IP address, your browser and the pages you open.
  • Call and form tracking. We use a call and form tracking service, WhatConverts, to see which pages and searches lead to calls and requests. It uses cookies and may log your visit, your IP address and how you found us. When you call or send the form, it can record the call details or the message with that information. It may also show a tracking phone number that forwards to ours.
  • Maps. The maps on this site come from Google. When one loads, Google can set its own cookies.

We only ask for what we need to help you.

Why do we collect it?

To answer you, book the job, do the work and keep a record of it. We don't use it for anything else without asking you first.

Who sees it?

Our team, and the services that handle it for us, such as our website host and the form service that delivers your message to us. WhatConverts sees what its tracking collects, and Google sees what its maps collect. We don't sell your information.

How long do we keep it?

As long as we need it to do the work and keep our records, and no longer. If you'd like it removed sooner, ask us.

What are my choices?

You can ask what we have on file, ask us to correct it, or ask us to remove it. You can also change your mind about how we use it.

Who do I ask?

Call (437) 410-8394 and ask for the person who looks after privacy, or write to us at Thorn Hill Gas Line Repair, 7163 Yonge St, Unit 260, Markham, ON L3T 0C8. If you're not happy with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

What if this changes?

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this page and its date.
