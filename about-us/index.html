ABOUT US

What do we do?

Thorn Hill Gas Line Repair is a gas, heating and cooling company serving Thornhill and the surrounding area, open 24 hours. It is licensed and insured.

We repair gas lines and gas appliances, and we repair, install and maintain furnaces, boilers, heat pumps, air conditioners and water heaters in Thornhill. Smell gas? Leave first, then call Enbridge, then call us.

Call (437) 410-8394

How a visit works

  1. 1

    You tell us what's happening. The smell, the sound, the notice, the appliance that won't light.

  2. 2

    We find the cause. It's the pipe, a fitting, a valve or the appliance itself.

  3. 3

    We quote the repair before we do the work.

  4. 4

    We fix it and replace what has failed.

  5. 5

    We pressure test the line. Gas doesn't go back on until the test passes.

  6. 6

    We check the appliance runs properly and tell you what we found.
