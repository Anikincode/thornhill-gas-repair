Ductless air conditioner repair finds why a mini-split isn't cooling or heating, whether that's a blocked drain, a dirty coil, a failed sensor, a communication fault between the indoor and outdoor units, or low refrigerant. In Thornhill we repair ductless systems and heat pump mini-splits.

This page covers common symptoms, why a mini-split leaks, what a blinking light or error code means and when a cleaning is enough.

What are common mini-split problems?

  • Water dripping from the indoor unit

  • Weak airflow or a musty smell

  • A blinking light or an error code

  • Not cooling, or the outdoor unit won't start

  • Cooling in summer but no heat in winter

Why is my mini-split leaking water?

Water with a hissing sound and weak cooling can point to a refrigerant leak, and that's a job for a technician.

  • A blocked, kinked or damaged drain line

  • A dirty filter that lets the coil freeze, then drip as it thaws

  • A cracked drain pan

  • An indoor unit that isn't level

What does a blinking light mean?

The unit is reporting a fault and has gone into protection mode. The code isn't the same on every brand, so note the blink pattern or the code, then call us with the make and model. Don't keep resetting the power, which can damage the unit.

Why isn't it cooling?

If none of those fit, it takes test equipment to check refrigerant and sensors.

  • It's set to Auto or Fan instead of Cool.

  • Many units wait a few minutes before starting.

  • The filter or coil is dirty.

  • The outdoor unit is blocked or iced over.

Is a cleaning enough?

Often, when the problem is airflow or a blocked drain. A technician can tell on the first visit.
