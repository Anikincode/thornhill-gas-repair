Thornhill Areas We Serve

Where we work in Thornhill, and the gas problems each part tends to have.

Thorn Hill Gas Line Repair serves all of Thornhill, on both the Vaughan and Markham sides of Yonge Street, from Steeles Avenue north. Older neighbourhoods tend to have aging pipe and original equipment, and newer ones have bigger houses with more gas appliances.

This page covers the neighbourhoods we serve, what's common in Thornhill homes, and which rules apply on each side of Yonge.

Which parts of Thornhill do you serve?

  • Heritage Thornhill (Thornhill village, Yonge Street and Centre Street). Some of the oldest houses in the area.
  • Uplands and Beverley Glen (Vaughan). Established neighbourhoods, many with houses from the 1970s and 1980s.
  • Brownridge and Crestwood-Springfarm-Yorkhill (Vaughan). Mostly 1960s to 1980s houses.
  • Thornhill Woods (Vaughan). Newer homes with several gas appliances each.
  • Royal Orchard and Bayview Glen (Markham). Large lots and larger houses, some rebuilt.
  • German Mills (Markham). Established houses east of Yonge.
  • Condo and townhouse areas along Yonge Street, Steeles Avenue and Bathurst Street. Work inside a unit, such as a gas range or fireplace, is possible. The building's own gas systems belong to the building's contractor.

If your street isn't listed, call us. We cover the whole of Thornhill.

What gas problems are common in Thornhill homes?

  • Aging pipe and equipment in houses from the 1960s to the 1990s. See gas line repair and red tag repair.
  • Many gas appliances per house, which means more joints to check. See gas appliance repair.
  • Pool heaters, outdoor kitchens and garage heaters on long added runs of pipe.
  • Renovations and rebuilds that need a pressure test.
  • Winter snow and ice on meters and vents. Keep both clear, as Enbridge asks.

Does it matter whether I'm in Vaughan or Markham?

Thornhill sits in two cities, split along Yonge Street. Gas work is regulated by TSSA across Ontario, so the same rule applies on both sides: it takes a registered fuels contractor. If your renovation needs a building permit, that comes from your city, Vaughan or Markham.
